Hogger Fodder explains why WoW's numbers behave the way they do. It doesn't tell you what to wear.

Each post starts from something in the game that seemed odd, and works out the mechanism behind it. The tools are maths and economics, because that's what I have: I'm an academic economist, and I tank. Caps turn out to be kinks, the attack table turns out to be a capital structure, rage turns out to be a feedback loop. Readers should leave thinking "I could have worked that out."

**Who it's for.** Players first. If you want the answer and the intuition, it's in the first two screens of every post. Theorycrafters and economists get the formal parts in collapsible boxes, so nobody has to read a derivation they didn't come for.

**On sims.** Not against them. A sim gives you the answer; reasoning tells you why, and when the answer will change.

**Pace.** There is no schedule. I also publish papers, and posts appear when something in the game invites one. Subscribe by [RSS](/index.xml) and you'll see them when they come.

**Corrections.** Every number is checked by hand before it goes up, and I still expect to be wrong sometimes. Comment below a post or [open an issue](https://github.com/hoggerfodder/hoggerfodder.github.io/issues). Corrections are folded in with credit in the revisions section at the bottom of each post, like acknowledgements in a paper. Logged data is welcome too: some coefficients can only be pinned down from a few thousand swings.

**On writing.** The ideas and the maths are mine. Drafting is AI-assisted, and I'd rather say so than have it be a gotcha. The series is about reasoning in the open; who typed the sentences is the least interesting part.

**Tips.** Posts are free. If one was worth something to you, there's a [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/hoggerfodder). It changes nothing about what gets written.

**Name.** Hogger is the gnoll in Elwynn Forest who has killed more level-eight characters than any boss in the game. Fodder is what he eats. It's also food for thought.

Hogger Fodder is an independent blog, not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.
